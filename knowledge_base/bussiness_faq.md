# Millet Milk Sales - Business FAQ

## Business Information

Business Name: Millet Milk Sales

Main Product: Ragi Milk

## Delivery Options

Customers can choose between:

1. Home Delivery
2. Customer Pickup

The customer should provide a delivery address
when choosing home delivery.

Specific delivery areas, delivery charges and
delivery timings must be confirmed by the business.

## Payment Options

Customers can choose:

1. Cash on Delivery
2. UPI

UPI payment is considered successful only after
payment verification.

## Ordering Process

Customers can ask about products, quantity,
delivery options and payment methods.

An order is created only after:
- Customer details are validated.
- Product availability is checked.
- The customer reviews the order summary.
- The customer confirms the order.

## Customer Support

Unclear questions, complaints and special requests
should be forwarded to human customer support.
