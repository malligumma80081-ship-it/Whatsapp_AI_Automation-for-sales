# Millet Milk Sales - Product Information

## Main Product

Product Name: Ragi Milk

## Available Product Sizes

1. Ragi Milk - 500 ml
2. Ragi Milk - 1 litre

## Product Catalogue Rules

The application maintains product availability,
stock quantities and current prices in its SQL database.

The SQL database is the authoritative source for
current product prices and stock.

The knowledge base must not be used as the source
of live prices or inventory information.

## Product Information Limitations

Do not invent:
- Ingredients.
- Nutritional values.
- Medical benefits.
- Shelf life.
- Storage instructions.
- Manufacturing details.

These details must be supplied and verified
by the business before being communicated.
